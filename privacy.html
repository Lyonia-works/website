Privacy

Last updated 12 October 2026

This site has no accounts, no cookies and no analytics. Fonts and images load from the site itself.

Visiting the site

The pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and browser details when you load a page.

The live badge on the home page asks a small service on Cloudflare whether Lyonia is streaming, about once a minute while the page is open. Cloudflare receives your IP address with each request. The service stores nothing about you.

Go-live notifications (only if you turn them on)

When you tap Notify me and allow notifications, your browser creates a push token: a random ID that lets notifications reach this browser.

  • The token goes to Google Firebase Cloud Messaging, which sends the notifications. Google also sees your IP address when you sign up.
  • Your browser keeps the token on your device, so the button can show that notifications are on.
  • Your browser's own push service delivers each notification: Google for Chrome and most Android browsers, Mozilla for Firefox, Apple for Safari.
  • A notification only says that Lyonia is live and what is being played.

We use the token only to send these notifications, based on your consent when you turn them on.

Turning notifications off

Tap Notifications on on the home page to turn them off; this removes your token from the notification list. You can also block notifications for this site in your browser's settings.

Your rights

You can ask what data relates to you and ask for it to be deleted. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.
